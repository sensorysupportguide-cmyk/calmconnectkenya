Hi! I'm Jane 👋
Founder of Calm & Connect Kenya

I know what it feels like to watch your child struggle — and feel completely helpless about what to do. The meltdowns. The refused meals. The sleepless nights. The school calls. The mornings that feel like battles before the day has even started.

"You are not failing. You are fighting. And this system is here to fight with you."

My search for answers led me to the gut-brain connection — and everything changed. When I discovered that 95% of serotonin is made in the gut, that the gut and brain communicate constantly, that what a child eats directly affects how they feel, focus and regulate — I knew I had found the missing piece.

But the information existed only in expensive books and Western protocols using foods most Kenyan families couldn't access. So I created Calm & Connect Kenya — a simple, realistic, Kenya-specific system using foods that are already in your kitchen.
